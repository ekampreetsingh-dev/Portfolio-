Hey There! I am Ekampreet Singh

I am Ekampreet Singh, a passionate writer and an emerging web developer and designer. For the past three years, I have immersed myself in the art of storytelling, crafting numerous poems and actively developing an original fiction project. Writing taught me the value of structure, tone, and pacing — skills that naturally sparked my curiosity for digital creation. Now, I channel that same creative drive into building clean, visually engaging, and user-friendly websites from the ground up.

Whether through lines of prose or lines of code, I am focused on creating work that connects with people.
